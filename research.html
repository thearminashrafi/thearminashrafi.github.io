Research

I study reinforcement learning — specifically how agents can discover, represent, and reuse behavioral knowledge. My current work spans latent policy spaces and sample-efficient experience replay, with a broader interest in continual learning and active inference.

Current Projects

  • Latent search over policy representations in grid-world environments
  • Coreset-based experience replay for improved sample efficiency
  • Continual learning and non-stationary agent dynamics (side interest)
