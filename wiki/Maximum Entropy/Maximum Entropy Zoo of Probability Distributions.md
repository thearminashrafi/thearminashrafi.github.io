The following is a list of derivations and posts on how to generate the various forms of common probability distributions, using the principle of maximum entropy, and how that illuminates interesting aspects regarding these distributions. Some derivations will be special cases of others, but are also treated in their own right, as I think there is added value in looking at the maximum entropy derivation directly, notably many of the distributions coming from the exponential family.
